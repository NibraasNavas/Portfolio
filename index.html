Computer Vision & Edge AI

AwareDrive: Real-Time Driver Drowsiness & Fatigue Detection

The Human Impact: Driver fatigue is a primary factor in over 20% of severe traffic collisions. AwareDrive provides continuous, non-invasive edge monitoring to save lives before micro-sleep occurs.

Engineered an embedded real-time computer vision system capable of monitoring driver alertness under variable lighting conditions. The system captures live video streams, applies 68-point facial landmark regression via Dlib and OpenCV, and computes Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) metrics to detect involuntary micro-sleep and repeated yawning.

  • Instant acoustic and visual alarm triggers on consecutive eye closure threshold violations (PERCLOS).
  • Standalone deployment on Raspberry Pi edge hardware with low latency processing.
  • Robust facial tracking resilient against head pose variations and brief occlusions.
Python TensorFlow OpenCV Dlib Raspberry Pi Real-Time Telemetry

Generative AI & Mobile

Recipe Wizard: Multimodal Vision-Powered AI Culinary Assistant

The Human Impact: Tackles household food waste and culinary decision fatigue by transforming random kitchen ingredients into healthy, tailored recipes in seconds.

Developed a cross-platform mobile application combining Google Gemini's multimodal vision capabilities with a responsive Flutter architecture. Users snap a photo of whatever is in their pantry or refrigerator; the system recognizes individual ingredients, takes into account dietary restrictions (e.g., vegan, gluten-free, low-carb), and generates step-by-step cooking instructions with nutritional breakdowns.

  • Multimodal vision prompt engineering leveraging the Gemini API for high accuracy ingredient extraction.
  • Offline-first recipe bookmarking and caching using Hive local storage.
  • Cloud synchronization and cross-device favorites management backed by Firebase.
Flutter Dart Gemini API Hive NoSQL Firebase Multimodal AI

Personal portfolio website of **Nibraas Navas** — Data Science & Machine Learning Engineer specializing in Computer Vision, predictive analytics, and edge AI applications.

---

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic
- **Palette**: Editorial luxury centered on **Smokey Blue (`#2D394A`)** and **Morlet Red (`#562025`)**.
- **Dual Themes**:
  - **Smokey Atelier (Dark Luxury Mode)**: Ambient glowing orbs, glassmorphism, and deep slate cards.
  - **Warm Editorial (Linen & Wine Light Mode)**: Parchment linen styling with rich wine accents.
- **Typography**: Paired Google Fonts (*Outfit*, *Newsreader Serif*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*).

---

## 🚀 Key Sections & Features
- **Asymmetric Editorial Hero**: Conversational manifesto, availability badge, live metrics, and floating context badges.
- **The Story & Mindset (Bento Grid)**: Personal journey narrative, 3 core engineering principles, and current research focus.
- **Craft & Toolkit Matrix**: Interactive tabbed competency filter across ML, Data Analytics, APIs, Visualization, and Tools.
- **Featured Case Studies**:
  - **AwareDrive**: Real-time driver drowsiness detection with OpenCV, Dlib 68-point facial landmarks, and Raspberry Pi edge deployment.
  - **Recipe Wizard**: Multimodal vision AI recipe generator built with Gemini API, Flutter, Hive, and Firebase.
- **Academic Trail**: Milestone journey covering B.Tech CSE (Data Science) at SCMS School of Engineering and Technology.
- **Interactive Contact Hub**: Preset starter chips for inquiry drafting, one-click email copying with toast notifications, and full resume download.
